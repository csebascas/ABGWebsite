A day on AI in business, at Michigan Ross

On October 23, 2026, AI Business Group hosted the first Michigan AI Business Conference: keynotes, panels and lunch sessions on how AI is changing business, with students and people from industry in the same room.

Jennifer Marsman of Microsoft gave the keynote. Panels brought in speakers from JPMorgan, Citi, Bank of America and Michigan’s venture community, and Jacob Walker of BNY and Josh Spiewak of NorthStar Advisory Group led sessions over lunch.

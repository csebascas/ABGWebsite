This semester

Drone Landing Site Detection

Airspace Experience Technologies, Inc. · Aug 2026 – Dec 2026

AIBG partnered with ASX to develop an AI vision prototype that detects and classifies objects near a drone landing site. The project also evaluates how well the prototype performs and recommends next steps toward operational readiness.

This semester

Space Asset Pricing Model

Space Finance Company · Aug 2026 – Dec 2026

AIBG partnered with Space Finance Company to develop research and a pricing model for financing space assets. The project starts with research on default risk and asset valuation, then builds a machine learning model on those findings.

This semester

Predictive Hand Hygiene Risk Scoring

BioVigil · Aug 2026 – Dec 2026

AIBG partnered with BioVigil to develop a predictive risk scoring layer for its hospital hand hygiene monitoring system. It is designed to flag emerging compliance risk early and give infection control teams a clear set of recommended actions.

This semester

Prediction Market Signal Analysis

Financial research firm · Aug 2026 – Dec 2026

AIBG partnered with a financial research firm to develop a prototype and written findings on whether prediction market data gives early or independent signals for its research. The work includes a back-testing framework and an interactive dashboard.

This semester

Competitor Feature Tracker

Clerked · Aug 2026 – Dec 2026

AIBG partnered with Clerked to develop an automated tool that tracks competitor feature releases in the accounts payable automation space. Once handed over, the tool keeps Clerked updated on new findings on an ongoing basis.

This semester

Social Travel Platform Features

Trippii · Aug 2026 – Dec 2026

AIBG partnered with Trippii to develop features for its social travel platform, including a feed ranking algorithm, an AI chatbot with memory, and travel booking integrations. The goal is to leave Trippii with working components it can keep building on.
